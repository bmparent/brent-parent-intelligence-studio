# Manual native-section recipe

Start with five hidden pages on Squarespace **7.1**. Build using native blocks and section controls; no existing page is automatically replaced. Content below is fictional sample copy, not a real service provider or verified result. Use your real service scope and approved photos. No testimonials, prices, emergency promises, service-area coverage, certifications, or customer results are supplied.

## Home

1. Blank section, two columns on desktop and one on mobile. Left: native Heading “A clear next step for the work ahead.” Body: “[Business name] helps [service area] plan [actual services]. Tell us what you have in mind.” Primary native Button: “Talk about your project” → your Contact page. Right: native Image block with your actual work photo and descriptive alt text. Do not put contact information only inside the photograph.
2. Native service list: three headings “Repairs”, “More comfortable spaces”, “Project planning”, each with short plain scope description and an ordinary text link to the relevant service detail. The optional `selector.html` may accompany this list; do not remove the native links.
3. Native process band: “Tell us what you need” → “Talk through the options” → “Decide on the next step”. Explain assessment/quote/payment steps only if accurate for your business.
4. Native call-to-action section linking Contact. Keep phone/email in native footer and verify both.

## Services

1. Heading “Services, explained.” Introduce the actual work you undertake.
2. One native section per service: Heading, body including scope/exclusions, optional photo, button to a service detail page. Preserve ordinary links to all services when any optional selector filters its own descriptions.
3. FAQ using native accordion: service area, inquiry response expectations, process, and costs if approved. Do not invent availability or a response guarantee.

## Service Detail

Duplicate a native page for each service you actually provide. Include service name, intended customer/task, inclusions, exclusions, typical next step, relevant image/alt text, and native Contact button. Replace demo context with fact-checked business copy. Native sections can be reordered without editing HTML.

## Work / Process

Show the three-step process in an editorial list. If you have permission to show completed work, add native Image blocks/captions and describe what was actually performed. Otherwise use process explanations and omit a project gallery. No sample business claim is evidence.

## Contact

Use a native Form block for name, reply email, optional telephone, service choice, and message. Configure storage/recipient and privacy/retention wording for your business. Include native direct email/phone and relevant hours. Test receipt from a permitted test address. A booking link requires a separately configured/verified service; this kit supplies no availability/calendar system.

## Editing and publication checklist

Change the business name, page title/meta description, copy, native image, alt text, link, and form recipient. Preview at a phone width with a long headline and incomplete image selection. Verify text still explains the service if an image fails. Check ordinary navigation, visible focus, contrast, comfortable touch targets, script-free content, form receipt, and external destinations. Publish only the test page, inspect signed out, then record actual evidence before production changes. Keep your former pages and saved Site Styles as rollback targets.
