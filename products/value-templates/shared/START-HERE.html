Before you upload
  • Replace fictional claims, example details and the demonstration footer with accurate business content. Never put passwords or secret keys in these files.
  • Compare your edited page with JavaScript disabled. Its fallback text should describe your business too. Check every page, navigation link, image and action on a phone and desktop, and by keyboard.
  • For an event, check that the date, time and location printed on the page match the downloaded calendar. UTC times include the date; account for your local time zone.
  • Inquiry tools prepare a local text or email draft. They do not send a message, make a reservation or process payment. If you need delivery through a web form, add and test that service separately.
  • Test your chosen colors for readable text and visible keyboard focus. This editor validates color format, not accessibility.
  • Update page titles and descriptions, and remove the robots noindex tag only when your real site is ready. Upload the required website files to a compatible host. Keep this setup page, setup.js, backups and license documents with your private working copy.
