# Agentic SEO Implementation Checklist

## Page and intent

- [ ] The page has one primary audience, intent, and conversion action.
- [ ] The main heading names the service/question in customer language.
- [ ] The first section gives an answer before background or brand language.
- [ ] Claims are supported with proof, examples, dates, or transparent limitations.
- [ ] The page is meaningfully distinct from other service/Insights pages.

## Entity and service clarity

- [ ] Eidos Works, Brent Parent, service, location/market, and contact relationships are consistent.
- [ ] Service scope, deliverables, audience, and next step are explicit.
- [ ] Important terms are explained without keyword stuffing.
- [ ] Contact, About, privacy, and business identity details are easy to find.

## Technical discoverability

- [ ] Canonical URL is correct and uses `https://eidos-works.com`.
- [ ] Title and meta description are unique and accurate.
- [ ] Page is linked in navigation, sitemap, or a relevant index.
- [ ] Robots directives do not block the page accidentally.
- [ ] Server-rendered HTML contains the core content.
- [ ] Status code, redirects, mobile layout, and performance are checked.

## Structured data

- [ ] Schema type matches visible content; no fabricated ratings or claims.
- [ ] Organization/Person/WebSite relationships are consistent.
- [ ] Service or Article fields are complete where appropriate.
- [ ] Breadcrumbs reflect real navigation.
- [ ] FAQ schema is used only for visible, useful FAQs and remains eligible under current search guidance.
- [ ] Structured data validates without critical errors.

## Content usefulness

- [ ] Headings make the answer structure easy to scan.
- [ ] Real questions, constraints, tradeoffs, process, and examples are addressed.
- [ ] Internal links connect related services, proof, Insights, and next actions.
- [ ] External citations point to authoritative primary sources where needed.
- [ ] “Updated” dates are accurate, not cosmetic.

## Agent-friendly UX

- [ ] Semantic HTML, real links/buttons, accessible labels, and visible focus states are present.
- [ ] Forms have predictable fields, validation, consent context, and confirmation states.
- [ ] Price/context is visible where it affects a decision.
- [ ] No essential content depends on animation, canvas, hover, or JavaScript-only interaction.

## Measurement

- [ ] Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools are configured where relevant.
- [ ] Crawl/index state and target queries/questions are recorded.
- [ ] Form/CTA conversions are measured without collecting unnecessary data.
- [ ] AI referral notes distinguish observed evidence from inference.
- [ ] A review/refresh date and owner are assigned.

## Required limitation

No one can guarantee AI citations, rankings, or instant traffic. The goal is a cleaner foundation: pages that are useful, crawlable, understandable, and easier to trust.
