# Client Handoff Checklist

## Project and acceptance

- [ ] Final scope and deliverables are reconciled against the approved SOW.
- [ ] Client acceptance, remaining exceptions, and completion date are recorded.
- [ ] Final invoice/payment status is confirmed.
- [ ] Before/after evidence and launch URL are saved.

## Accounts and ownership

- [ ] Domains, hosting, analytics, repositories, design files, email, integrations, and vendor accounts have named owners.
- [ ] Client has administrator access through their own account.
- [ ] Temporary Eidos Works access is removed or assigned an expiration/review date.
- [ ] Credentials were transferred through an approved secure channel, never a Doc or Sheet.
- [ ] Billing ownership and renewal dates are clear.

## Deliverables and documentation

- [ ] Canonical source files and exported assets are organized and linked.
- [ ] Content-editing, deployment, backup, and rollback instructions are current.
- [ ] Environment variables/integration names are documented without secret values.
- [ ] Data flow, automation triggers, failure states, and manual fallback are documented.
- [ ] Accessibility, SEO/schema, analytics, and performance checks are recorded.

## Training

- [ ] Client can complete the most common update.
- [ ] Client can recognize and report an error with useful evidence.
- [ ] Client knows what not to change and why.
- [ ] Recording, notes, or live training were delivered if included.

## Known limitations and maintenance

- [ ] Platform/browser/device limitations are explicit.
- [ ] Third-party quotas, subscriptions, and dependencies are listed.
- [ ] No ranking, AI citation, traffic, uptime, or revenue guarantee is implied.
- [ ] Maintenance owner, cadence, price/scope, and support boundary are clear.
- [ ] Privacy/security responsibilities after handoff are assigned.

- [ ] Project tracker status is Complete or Maintenance.
