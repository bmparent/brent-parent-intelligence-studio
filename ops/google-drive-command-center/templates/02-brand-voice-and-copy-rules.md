# Brand Voice and Copy Rules

## Eidos Works in one sentence

Eidos Works designs and builds clearer websites, practical automations, decision dashboards, and AI-ready search foundations for businesses that need their digital systems to work harder.

## Voice

- Clear, capable, observant, and direct.
- Explain the business outcome before the implementation detail.
- Sound like an experienced collaborator, not a trend-chasing agency.
- Use concrete proof, constraints, and next steps.
- Keep technical language only where it helps a customer decide.

## Avoid

- Empty superlatives such as “revolutionary,” “cutting-edge,” or “game-changing.”
- AI filler, inflated promises, fake urgency, and invented metrics.
- Copy that describes the interface to its creator instead of helping the customer.
- Guarantees of rankings, AI citations, traffic, or revenue.
- Treating Eidos Brain research as a finished commercial product.

## Naming

- **Eidos Works** is the public studio/business.
- **Brent Parent** is the founder and principal behind the work.
- **Eidos Brain** is a research/proof-stage intelligence architecture case study or lab area.
- **Eidos Snapshot** is the focused paid website assessment.

## Structure pattern

1. Answer the customer’s question in the heading or first sentence.
2. Explain why the issue matters to their business.
3. Show what Eidos Works actually does.
4. Support the claim with proof, an example, or a transparent limitation.
5. Offer one specific next step.

## Agentic SEO language

Preferred: “We structure pages so customers, search engines, and AI assistants can understand your services, proof, and next steps.”

Required limitation: “No one can guarantee AI citations, rankings, or instant traffic. The goal is to build a cleaner foundation: pages that are useful, crawlable, understandable, and easier to trust.”

## Final copy check

- [ ] Does the first paragraph answer a real customer question?
- [ ] Are claims specific and supportable?
- [ ] Is the next action obvious?
- [ ] Can a non-technical owner understand it in one read?
- [ ] Has internal/process commentary been removed from customer-facing copy?
