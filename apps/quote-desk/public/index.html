Before you rely on an estimate

Is this a promise of a production time?

No. Speed, thread breaks, design complexity and operator work vary. This is a transparent planning model. Time real jobs and adjust the assumptions.

How are multiple heads and operators handled?

Machine cycles equal quantity divided by active heads, rounded up. Extra operators divide handling time only. Setup, stitching and handling are added sequentially; the estimate takes no credit for overlapping work.

What happens when I cancel?

Normal cancellation takes effect at the end of your paid period. You can read, export or delete saved records afterwards. New saves require active paid access.

Does this digitize artwork or connect to my machines?

No. Enter stitch count from your design software. The tool creates estimates and planning records; it does not control a machine, digitize artwork or guarantee a profitable sale.
