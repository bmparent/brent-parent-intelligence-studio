# Agentic SEO implementation

Eidos Works treats agentic SEO as foundational website work for discovery through people, search engines, and AI assistants. It is not presented as a special ranking mechanism.

## Implemented foundation

- Prerendered semantic HTML for the homepage, service page, Insights hub, and every article
- Page-specific titles, descriptions, canonicals, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata
- Canonical domain fixed to `https://eidos-works.com`
- Organization, Person, WebSite, Service, Article, and BreadcrumbList structured data where visible content supports it
- Clean internal links between services, Snapshot, Insights, and contact paths
- Generated sitemap, RSS feed, robots rules, and an accurate optional `llms.txt` index
- Real headings, buttons, links, labels, focus states, and reduced-motion behavior
- Private Snapshot result and processing routes excluded from indexing

## Content rules

Every public page should answer at least one customer question: what this is, who it helps, why it matters, what problem it solves, or what happens next. Claims must be tied to visible proof; rankings, AI citations, leads, and conversion gains are never guaranteed.

## Measurement after deployment

1. Verify `eidos-works.com` in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Submit `https://eidos-works.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Monitor indexing, crawl errors, branded/non-branded queries, form completions, and Snapshot starts.
4. Tag and review AI-referral traffic when referrer data is available.
5. Revisit high-value service pages using real customer questions and inquiry data.

`llms.txt` is maintained only as a compact site index. It is not required by Google and should not be marketed as a ranking hack.
