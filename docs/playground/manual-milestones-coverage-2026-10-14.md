## Remaining gates and explicitly optional work

- **External acceptance:** representative physical devices (including actual touch/GPU behavior), actual display presentation latency, and 6–8 unfamiliar target users. Viewport tests and headless timing cannot establish these.
- **Release integration:** mirror the changed renderer graph, build, run CI and verify the next paired hosted candidate. Keep the exact deployed source/bundle identity separate from this working-tree evidence.
- **Optional after dependable core:** multi-selection, reusable components and snapping. The handoff explicitly makes the first two conditional; this patch does not present them as completed.
- **No invented broad capability:** general grid-track authoring, arbitrary page absolute positioning, per-element standalone animation timelines, multipage output and host-specific integrations are not claimed.

The initial audit found two concrete source gaps—selected-element motion and visual guides—and untested current flows for asset-tray artwork, real focus/project-switch cancellation, parent selection and ordinary touch scroll. The source gaps now have bounded implementations; focused acceptance records their actual behavior. M1/M2 should be described as locally implemented with explicit release/device/user gates, rather than universally accepted or production-ready.
