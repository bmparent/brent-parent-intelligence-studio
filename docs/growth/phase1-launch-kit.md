## First week

1. Publish one founder introduction on LinkedIn.
2. Share the Central Florida page in one relevant group that permits it.
3. Send five individual notes to appropriate existing contacts.
4. Respond to interested people with the follow-up link; keep the discussion useful and specific.
5. Read `npm run growth:report -- --days=7`. Compare sources and form starts with provider-confirmed submissions, then decide where another conversation is warranted.

Do not interpret a handful of visits as a reliable conversion benchmark. Report replies and qualified conversations separately from site telemetry. None of these posts or messages has been sent automatically.
