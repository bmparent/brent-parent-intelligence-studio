## Proposed release and safe sequence

The public service pages, Insights hierarchy/search, glass navigation and source-based concept demonstrations passed this isolated hosted smoke. The integrated account, kit checkout/recovery, cloud Playground and AI features are **not yet approved for release**. Keep `EIDOS_PLAYGROUND_AI_ENABLED=false`, `EIDOS_PLAYGROUND_AUTHORING_ENABLED=false`, `SNAPSHOT_GENERATION_ENABLED=false`, `SNAPSHOT_CAPTURE_APPROVED=false`, `SNAPSHOT_PUBLIC_ENABLED=false`, and production paid/shop activation off until their own gates pass. The isolated preview shop uses TEST mode only. Preserve research behavior and private PERNR/Promo material.

After paired acceptance and explicit production approval: (1) capture production backend/site deployment IDs, main SHAs, schema backups and binding fingerprints; (2) dry-run and apply **additive** member/credential/Playground migrations to the correct production database without deleting rows or changing ledgers; (3) deploy and smoke the backend first with source revision, readiness, old and new contract probes; (4) deploy the approved site commit by the existing Pages release workflow, checking project/domain/config fingerprint and delivered assets; (5) smoke canonical `/`, three services, `/insights`, `/playground/`, account and controlled purchase recovery on the actual production pair. A `main` merge can itself deploy, so do not merge before the approved release window.

On failure, turn off new feature flags and restore the prior site/backend deployment identities. Keep additive columns/tables, member identities, order and entitlement rows, archive bytes, budget reservations and the Wellway ledger. Reconcile any in-flight checkout or webhook before retrying; never reset customer data or replay an uncertain paid provider request. Snapshot remains off through rollback.
