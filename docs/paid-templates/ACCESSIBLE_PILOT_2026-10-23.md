# Accessible website pilot

Owner decision: Brent Parent, October 23, 2026.

## Approved first product and goal

Switchboard Developer 1.0.3 is approved at **$25 USD, one time**, replacing the proposed $99 price for new purchases when this candidate is released. This decision approves the price and product direction; production activation still follows the current release acceptance.

Aim for **4–8 paid customer transactions per month** during the pilot. At $25 that is $100–$200 gross before payment fees, refunds and support or delivery costs; it is a learning target, not a forecast. Keep TEST activity separate from paid customer results.

Only this edition changes price. The existing Cinematic Starter and other draft editions keep their own prices and acceptance. Existing orders retain their original amount, edition, version, archive hash and entitlements. Review later increases against observed customer value and support effort; publish changes for future purchases.

## Customer promise

Give people with limited budgets or access to AI a high-quality functional website they can own, personalize and publish on their chosen host. Keep the offer a one-time purchase with clear contents, ownership and optional third-party costs. No Eidos subscription is part of this pilot.

The delivered website should work independently of Eidos or AI access. Its HTML, navigation, content, responsive layout, accessibility and search metadata must serve the customer's visitors. Discoverability is part of the publishing checklist; search traffic is not an included or guaranteed result.

The current Developer package still requires HTML/CSS/JavaScript editing. Do not describe that package as an automatic no-code service. Before promoting a beginner-ready route, observe a person with limited technical experience replace business details and images, publish the files to a host, open the public URL on a phone and complete the intended contact action. Explain clearly that the current contact form prepares an email draft rather than sending a message.

## Pilot operations and customer learning

Keep the existing one-site license, 30 days of installation guidance and 90 days of supplied-file defect corrections visible. Custom design, content entry, hosting, domain setup and third-party connections need an explicit separate scope. Do not expand support into unlimited custom development.

Track paid transactions and unique customers; successful download, recovery and publication; setup time and where buyers need help; delivered customer feedback; support time, actual fees, refunds and reasons; repeat visits and repeat purchases. Ask for honest feedback after the customer has used the product. Obtain permission before publishing identifiable feedback or work.

Brent and Eidos work together on clear product explanations, onboarding, support, defect triage and follow-through. The customer's observed result determines completion. Optional agent connections, community participation and AI tools should provide independent practical value to advanced users without making the basic purchased website depend on them. Existing model budgets and feature gates remain in effect.

Brent reports little current cash overhead beyond assistant interactions. Record actual payment, delivery and support costs as observed, and keep unknown costs unknown; this decision does not approve new spending or claim a measured profit margin.

## Release coordination

Pricing work is isolated from site PR139 and backend PR90. Carry the same server price into both repositories, update the public catalog and release manifests, and pass the price-agreement checks before integration. Keep the 1.0.3 archive immutable; a price change is not a new file version. Existing purchase, recovery and refund evidence remains scoped to its original amount and environment.

The physical-device, screen-reader, browser-zoom, provider and production acceptance tasks retain their current owner and evidence. This pilot decision does not enable LIVE sales, send customer messages, change provider configuration or waive release gates.
