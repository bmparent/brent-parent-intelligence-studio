Ask about the house ↗
  • Rooms & capacityAdd the actual bedroom layout, bed sizes, and maximum permitted occupancy.
  • AccessAdd step-free routes, doorway measurements, bathroom details, and parking information. Do not claim full accessibility without checking.
  • IncludedList actual linens, kitchen equipment, connectivity, and outdoor space.
  • Before you bookState your check-in times, cancellation policy, deposit terms, and whether taxes or fees are additional.
